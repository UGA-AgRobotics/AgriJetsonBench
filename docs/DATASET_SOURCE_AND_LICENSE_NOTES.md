# Dataset source and licence notes

The original image/mask dataset packages are not included. Locked splits, class
metadata, scientific logs and evaluation snapshots are retained. In particular,
ten archived evaluation records contain hash-identical copies of the full
1,112-image/1,886-annotation COCO ground truth used by the aligned accuracy
comparison. Other saved annotation/prediction records may also be present in
the raw deployment evidence. They remain subject to their original rights.
See `provenance/supplement_asset_availability.csv` for confirmed matching files.

The original package's source and licence notes are retained unchanged in
`historical_pre_revision_metadata/DATASET_SOURCE_AND_LICENSE_NOTES.md`.
Those notes identify CottonWeedDet12 with Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7535814 and
CC-BY-NC-4.0, and CropAndWeed with the cropandweed/cropandweed-dataset repository
and non-commercial/no-redistribution terms. Obtain the original datasets through
their sources and comply with their applicable terms. These are recorded source
attributions, not new grants of permission or a licence for this entire release.

Repository code/documentation retains its existing MIT licence. Third-party
notices, dataset attributions and manuscript-source dependency notices remain
in their respective locations. Historical suggested Zenodo metadata is retained
for context; no new package-wide licence or identifier is assigned here.
